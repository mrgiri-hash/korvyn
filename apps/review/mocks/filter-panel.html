What to look at

1. The two panels are the same object. Scroll them side by side: the Context band and the Presentation band match field for field. That is what makes it read as one panel rather than a different panel per screen.

2. Band two is where the flux drawer went. Review scope, Reviewer status, the two switches, Group rows by, Sections shown, Columns & view — all of it, plus the engine's own suggestion card at the top, which only appears when a review is open.

3. The inheritance line is the load-bearing part. Every preference says Using global or Overridden for this review, with one click back. Without it a per-page override becomes state nobody can explain.

4. Absent, not greyed. The Trial Balance has no statement grid, so the grid controls are not there at all — band two is per-page by definition, so nothing has to be disabled to explain itself.
